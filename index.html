About Me

I am a Research Associate-I at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar, working within the ANRF-CRG funded project titled "Twisted Links and Twisted Virtual Knot" under the guidance of Dr. Madeti Prabhakar.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune under the supervision of Prof. Rama Mishra, where my doctoral thesis focused on the Parameterization of Knotted Surfaces Arising from Classical and Welded Knots.

Research Interests

My primary research architecture resides in low-dimensional topology and knot theory, focusing closely on:

  • Surface knot theory and knots on thickened surfaces.
  • Virtual, twisted, and welded knot theories.
  • Hyperbolic knot theory.
