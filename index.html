About Me

I am a postdoctoral research fellow at the University of Science, working alongside the Topology and Geometry Group. I completed my Ph.D. at Institute of Geometry under the supervision of Prof. Henri Poincaré.

My research lies at the intersection of low-dimensional topology and knot theory. Specifically, I study quantum knot invariants, Khovanov homology, and smooth 4-manifold invariants via trisection and surgery theory.

Research Interests

My current projects focus on the following themes:

  • Smooth Concordance: Investigating the structural properties of the knot concordance group \(\mathcal{C}\).
  • Grid Diagrams: Developing computational algorithms to optimize grid index variants for exceptional link families.
  • Categorification: Extending link homologies to virtual and welded knot configurations.
