About Me

Hello! Welcome to my homepage!

My name is Tumpa Mahato, and I am currently a Research Associate-I in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Ropar, working on the ANRF-CRG project "Twisted Links and Twisted Virtual Knot" supervised by Dr. Madeti Prabhakar.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune under the supervision of Prof. Rama Mishra. My doctoral thesis was titled "Parameterization of Knotted Surfaces Arising from Classical and Welded Knots".

My primary area of interest lies in Knot Theory and Low-dimensional topology. More specifically, my research explores different knotted structures in three and four dimensions.

Research

My ongoing mathematical work explores structural developments in knot spaces across multiple areas:

  • Surface Knot Theory: Investigating the structural parameters and topological layout maps of knotted surfaces.
  • Virtual & Twisted Knot Invariants: Studying invariants like the $Q^z$ polynomial adjustments for exceptional link spaces.
  • Computational Modeling: Developing algorithmic implementations. Notably, our companion Mathematica workspace tracking "Polynomially Knotted 2-spheres" was officially chosen and showcased in the "Publication Materials" and "Staff Picks" columns by Wolfram.
